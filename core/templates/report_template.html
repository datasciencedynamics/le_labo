Method and limits

Stores. __STORE_NOTE__

Demographics. County population, density, median household income, college attainment, foreign-born share, age mix and rural share from ACS 2012–2016 five-year estimates (MIT Election Data and Science Lab county file). Land area from Census county boundaries.

Model. L2-penalized logistic regression on standardized features. Scores are out-of-fold predictions averaged over 20 repeats of stratified 5-fold cross-validation, so no county is scored by a model that saw its own label.

Reading it. Population dominates, so the AUC mostly reflects that big urban counties have stores. Income enters negatively after controlling for education: Le Labo sits in dense, educated urban cores, and wealthier suburban counties score lower. County is coarse; a real site study would work at tract or neighborhood level with foot-traffic data.
