How to read this map

The score. Each county's score says how closely it resembles the counties where Le Labo already has boutiques. It is a similarity score, not a sales forecast: it cannot tell you how much a store would sell.

Open and fill-in markets. Open markets score high and have no boutique within __RADIUS__ miles; they are the new-market candidates. Fill-in markets score high but already sit near a boutique, so a store there would deepen a metro Le Labo already serves.

Where the data comes from. __STORE_NOTE__ County figures come from the US Census Bureau's American Community Survey (2012–2016 averages) and the 2010 Census.

Keep in mind. The profile is learned from where Le Labo has chosen to open, so it repeats past choices and can miss markets the brand never tried. Counties are large and varied inside, and the population figures predate recent moves. Use this as a shortlist for closer study, such as neighborhood visits and foot-traffic data, not as a site decision. How the model works and how well it performs is under Model Info & Performance.

How it works

The label. A county is positive if it hosts a standalone Le Labo boutique (__N_STORES__ boutiques in __N_COUNTIES__ counties). The model therefore learns where Le Labo has chosen to open, not consumer demand or store performance.

The model. __M_HOW__

Map scores. The tuned model saw 60% of counties while fitting, so its own predictions on those counties are in-sample. Every score on the map instead comes from the tuned pipeline refit out-of-fold: __REPEATS__ repeats of stratified 5-fold cross-validation with sigmoid calibration inside each fold, averaged. No county is scored by a model that saw its label.

Why the large-county figures. Every store county is a large urban one, so across all counties population alone already reaches an AUC near 0.99. The test of the model is how it ranks counties of 500k or more, where siting decisions are made, against population alone.

Data. Demographics are ACS 2012–2016 five-year estimates (MIT Election Data and Science Lab county file); rural share is from the 2010 Census; land area and county boundaries from the Census Bureau. Stores: __STORE_NOTE__

Validation. Each store county is hidden in turn, the pipeline is refit, and the hidden county is ranked against every county without a store. A low rank means the model would have flagged that market before Le Labo opened there.
